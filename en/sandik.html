SANDIK BAŞINA

Campaign loudly. Vote secretly. Win unexpectedly.

A darkly humorous strategy game about elections, power, and uncertainty. Four party leaders campaign across the country, trying to win as many cities as possible before election day.

You don't just play the map — you play the system.

👥 4 Players
⏱️ 90 Minutes
🎂 Ages 16+
🎯 Strategy

What Is This Game?

Sandık Başına is a strategy game where political campaigns are public, but election results are hidden until the very end. Players place ballot boxes, launch campaigns, disrupt rivals, and try to control the map — without ever knowing the true balance of power.

Each player represents a political party leader.

Over 12 rounds, players campaign, place ballot boxes, and spend resources.

Ballot boxes are placed openly, but their vote values remain hidden.

Campaigns disrupt opponents, shift control, and create chaos.

What Makes It Different?

In Sandık Başına, campaigns are public — but votes are secret.
