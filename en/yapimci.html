Yapımcı Arıyorum

Sell your story. Survive the industry.

There's a grumpy but wealthy producer at the table. You're a screenwriter trying to sell a movie idea. Use Genre, Character, and Movie Title cards to pitch the weirdest story imaginable — and convince the producer to fund it.

👥 2–10 Players
⏱️ 30–60 min
🎂 Ages 12+
🎲 Party / Storytelling

What Is This Game?

A storytelling party game where one player becomes a moody film producer, and the others are desperate screenwriters. Your goal isn't to have the best idea — it's to tell the most convincing story. (Tested multiple times with real people who argued a lot).

One player is the producer. Everyone else is a screenwriter.

Genre, Character, and Movie Title cards define your pitch.

The screenwriter creates and tells a full movie story.

The producer chooses the story that "smells like money."

What Makes It Different?

The producer can interrupt. Other screenwriters can interfere. The more absurd the intervention, the better. This game rewards improvisation, pressure, and chaos — just like the film industry.
